Cross-agency storm scenario

The final exercise for the first year simulated a coastal storm that hit East Market, Bayview, and North Pier at the same time. Heavy rain pushed water toward Old Canal Pump Station, a grid outage affected Bayview Hospital, and evacuation demand increased at North Pier Shelter.

In the scenario, BlueRiver Water Authority issued a flood alert for Old Canal Pump Station. HarborGrid Energy switched Bayview Hospital into microgrid island mode and reported battery reserve through the common event schema. MetroLink Transit activated Route 7 service from East Market Depot to Bayview Hospital and North Pier Shelter. Civic Data Trust published signed data packages from all three agencies on the Open Harbor Portal.

The exercise revealed why the program uses an attributed, cross-agency view of the city. The hospital microgrid mattered to transit because Route 7 carried medical staff. The pump station mattered to the shelter because flooded streets could block access. The common event schema mattered to all agencies because it gave responders a shared incident timeline.

The review board concluded that the strongest evidence of progress came from multi-agency dependencies, not from isolated project completion. The next phase will test whether Route 7 detours, Bayview Hospital microgrid status, and Old Canal Pump Station alerts can be interpreted together during a live storm.
